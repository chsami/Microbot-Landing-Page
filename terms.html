What Microbot is

Microbot is a free, open-source client for Old School RuneScape, built on RuneLite. It includes scripts and plugins that automate parts of the game. You can also install plugins written by community developers from the Plugin Hub. Microbot Cloud is optional: it lets you link your clients and launchers to an online dashboard where you can watch your scripts, get alerts and control them remotely.

Read this before you use Microbot

Jagex's rules for Old School RuneScape don't allow third-party software that automates gameplay. Using Microbot can get your game account banned, temporarily or permanently, and you can lose everything on it. We can't prevent this, and we don't promise that any feature avoids detection.
