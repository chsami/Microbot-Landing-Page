Microbot 2.6.41

RuneLite 1.13.4 and the Zeal prayer

Microbot 2.6.41 moves the client to RuneLite 1.13.4. RuneLite's Prayer and XP Drop plugins now know about the new Zeal prayer, and the Party plugin shares your status with fewer messages. This page also covers fixes to three Hub plugins that came out earlier today: PestControl, MotherlodeMine and HSBlackJack. Your plugins and settings work exactly as before.

Zeal in the Prayer and XP Drop plugins

Once you unlock Zeal, it takes Piety's place in your prayer book. If you have the Prayer plugin's Boost indicator option on, an active Zeal now gets its own infobox, and hovering it shows its bonuses: +25% Attack, +28% Strength and +25% Defence. Before this update, the client didn't know about Zeal, so it got no infobox.

The XP Drop plugin can colour each drop by the prayer you have on, using its Melee, Range and Mage prayer color settings. Say you set melee drops to red. With Zeal on, your drops used to show in the Standard color as if no prayer was active. Now they're red. The same goes for Deadeye, which now uses your range colour, and Mystic Vigour, which now uses your mage colour.

Party plugin

The Party plugin now sends your stats (including hitpoints and prayer points), run and special attack energy, inventory, gear, active prayers and location to your party in combined updates, instead of a separate message for each. The party panel and the status overlay above your teammates look and work the same as before.

The message format changed, so everyone in the party needs the update. If you party with a friend who still runs Microbot 2.6.40 or an older RuneLite, you may not see each other's stats until you're both up to date.

Plugin updates

PestControl2.5.9

Say your Style 1 loadout is melee, with an Abyssal tentacle and the Slash attack style. The tentacle's combat buttons say Flick, Lash and Deflect, not Slash, so the plugin never found the style. It sat at INITIALISING and did nothing. It now matches styles by damage type: Flick, Lash, Chop and Hack count as Slash, Lunge and Jab as Stab, and Pound, Pummel and Smash as Crush.

If your Style 1 weapon isn't worn or in your inventory, the status now says why it's waiting, for example Ranged: missing weapon Adamant crossbow. If it still can't get ready after 15 seconds, it stops and tells you what to change, instead of waiting forever.

MotherlodeMine1.9.10

If you logged out, hopped or loaded a new area while the plugin was waiting on a vein, it threw an error and stopped waiting early. Now it keeps waiting.

HSBlackJack1.0.3

Your position is briefly unknown while you log in, hop or load a new area. The plugin used to stop with an error at those moments. Now it skips that moment and carries on.

How to update

Restart Microbot to get client 2.6.41. There's no new launcher this time. Plugin updates show up in the Microbot Hub panel inside the client. Something not working? Tell us on Discord.
