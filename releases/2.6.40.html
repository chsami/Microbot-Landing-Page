Microbot 2.6.40

Happy Halloween

Microbot is dressed up for Halloween. From 20 October to 1 November, the website, the launcher and the client's loading screen all get bats and jack-o'-lanterns. They turn off again on their own on 2 November and come back next October. This release ships Microbot client 2.6.40 (on RuneLite 1.13.3) and launcher 3.2.18. Your plugins and settings work exactly as before.

The client

Start Microbot and the loading window shows the Halloween logo: the usual Microbot logo with two bats circling it and two glowing pumpkins at its feet. Everything else on the loading screen is the same, from the progress bar to the tip of the day.

Launcher 3.2.18

A small jack-o'-lantern sits on the corner of the Microbot logo in the sidebar, and the line under "Microbot" says Happy Halloween. Three bats flap in the top corner, and the Play buttons turn pumpkin orange. If you leave the launcher open overnight, it notices when the season starts or ends at midnight, so you don't need to restart it.

The website

On themicrobot.com, the logo gets the same bats and pumpkins, and a cobweb hangs in the corner of the page header while bats fly across it. On the home page, two pumpkins glow in the bottom corner. The launcher shows the same page in its main area, so you see it there too. If your device is set to reduce motion, the bats and pumpkins stay still.

How to update

Restart Microbot to get client 2.6.40. The next time you open the launcher it says a new version is available: click Install to get 3.2.18, or download it from the home page. Happy Halloween, and tell us on Discord if anything looks off.
